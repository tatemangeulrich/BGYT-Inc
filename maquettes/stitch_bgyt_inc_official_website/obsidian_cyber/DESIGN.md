## Brand & Style

The design system projects a high-caliber video game studio and digital production house operating at the bleeding edge of interactive entertainment. The emotional target is authoritative, cinematic, and technically uncompromising—evoking the tension, precision, and immersive weight of next-generation engines.

The aesthetic fuses sleek minimalism with refined cyber-futurism and controlled glassmorphism:
- **Base Atmosphere:** An abyssal dark canvas using deep obsidian tones rather than neutral greys, creating an infinite void where digital artifacts emerge with stark clarity.
- **Accents:** Restrained electric cyan and high-energy neon blues utilized strategically as energetic conduits, laser focus markers, and interactive states rather than background washes.
- **Surface Quality:** Translucent obsidian slabs with microscopic border luminescence, razor-thin framing lines, and controlled luminous ambient flares.
- **Execution:** Zero extraneous decorative noise or generic neon clutter; every pixel serves brand stature, game title prestige, press transparency, and talent recruitment.

## Colors

The palette establishes an ultra-high contrast hierarchy tailored strictly for dark mode. Light mode is explicitly omitted to preserve the visual weight and cinematic theater expected of game development showcases.

- **Background Void (`#05070B`):** The absolute foundational background. Used across page viewports and full-bleed framing.
- **Obsidian Containers (`#0B0F19`, `#111827`):** Elevated structural containers and modular cards, maintaining high contrast against foreground copy while providing structural warmth.
- **Primary Kinetic Accent (`#00D2FF`):** The primary signal light. Used for focal CTAs, live deployment status badges, key metrics, and dynamic hover triggers.
- **Secondary Engine Blue (`#0066FF`):** The deep digital current. Used for primary button backdrops, active tabs, and gradient basepoints.
- **Tertiary Cyan Highlight (`#38BDF8`):** Fine details, micro-labels, focus rings, and border highlights.
- **Text & Surface Contrast:**
  - `High Contrast Text`: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) for primary titles, editorial leads, and crucial data readouts.
  - `Muted Text / Cool Silver`: Slate Silver (`#94A3B8`) for body prose, meta specifications, and technical copy.
  - `Ghost Borders`: Low-opacity electric blue overlays (`rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.12)`) and edge lines (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`).

## Typography

The typographic system balances futuristic studio branding with high-density editorial legibility:
- **Headlines (Space Grotesk):** Provides a technological, geometric voice with distinct character cuts that instantly signal game architecture, AAA releases, and visionary corporate initiatives.
- **Body & Longform (Geist):** Delivers clean, neutral, hyper-legible text for press briefings, career breakdowns, and technical lore logs. Maintains supreme readability even at high visual density.
- **Badges, Metrics, & Systems (JetBrains Mono):** Injects an authentic development console feel. Every category tag, release timestamp, build version, and platform specification leverages uppercase monospaced rhythm to anchor the high-tech studio reality.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is driven by an assertive, structured 12-column responsive fluid grid with disciplined vertical rhythm:

- **Canvas Limits:** Max content container width is pinned at `1440px` to maintain optimal scanning paths for production showcases and media carousels.
- **Grid Structure:**
  - **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns with `1.5rem` gutters and `3rem` boundary margins. Full-width features may break out for cinematic viewports.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns with `1.5rem` gutters and `2rem` outer margins.
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` outer margins. High-density stat clusters condense into 2x2 grids; media elements retain a 16:9 cinematic aspect ratio.
- **Component Flow:** Spacing follows a strict base-8 scale (`space-xs` through `space-xl`). Micro-alignments use `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px), while modular content blocks prioritize wide breathing room with `space-xl` (40px) to prevent cognitive overload amidst glowing elements.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is constructed through luminous layering, backdrop dispersion, and laser-precise perimeter lighting rather than natural directional drop shadows:

- **Base Layer (Ground Zero):** `#05070B` matte obsidian backdrop. Non-interactive.
- **Layer 1 (Card & Frame Slabs):** Semi-transparent `#0B0F19` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and a subtle interior 1px border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.07)`.
- **Layer 2 (Elevated & Active Surfaces):** Modals, flyout menus, and active game tiles elevate with `background: rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.85)` and an accent top-edge hairline in `rgba(0, 210, 255, 0.4)`.
- **Luminous Cyber Shadows:** Hovering over actionable entities triggers ambient, zero-spread blue flares (`box-shadow: 0 0 24px -4px rgba(0, 210, 255, 0.25)`). Hard drop shadows are forbidden.

## Shapes

The geometric architecture is tight, crisp, and surgical. By utilizing a soft, controlled radius (`roundedness: 1` = 0.25rem / 4px base), UI components retain a precision-engineered silhouette.

- **Standard Elements (4px):** Inputs, standard buttons, project cards, and badge frames.
- **Structural Containers (`rounded-lg` / 8px):** Hero banners, embedded video viewports, and primary modal panels.
- **Detail Chamfers:** Where brand accents demand, subtle 45-degree corner micro-notches (via CSS clip-paths) may be applied to featured game cards to reinforce an industrial, cyber-hardware motif. Full circular pill radius is strictly reserved for small status pings and icon badges.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Cyber CTA:** High-gloss gradient fill from `#0066FF` to `#00D2FF`, text `#05070B` in bold `Space Grotesk`, 4px border radius. Hover introduces a vivid cyan exterior aura (`0 0 20px rgba(0, 210, 255, 0.5)`) and micro-lift of -1px.
- **Secondary Ghost:** 1px border in `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.35)`, background in `rgba(11, 15, 25, 0.6)` with backdrop blur. Text in pure `#FFFFFF`. Hover shifts border to solid `#00D2FF` with internal cyan tint.
- **Action Links:** Monospaced `JetBrains Mono` label with an animated right arrow indicator that shifts +4px on hover.

### High-Tech Cards (Projects & Games)
- Background: `#0B0F19` with a subtle linear-gradient overlay from top-left (`rgba(0, 210, 255, 0.05)`) to bottom-right (`transparent`).
- 1px hairline perimeter border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`).
- Media headers maintain 16:9 ratio with an ambient bottom gradient vignette to blend cleanly into the card body.
- On hover, the top border transitionally illuminates to `#00D2FF`, accompanied by a subtle 1.01x media zoom.

### Badges & Status Chips
- Built using `label-badge` styling (`JetBrains Mono`, uppercase).
- Structure: 1px border matching the accent color, padded `4px 10px`, with a pulsating dot indicator for live builds (e.g., "LIVE BUILD // 0.9.4" or "ALPHA RECRUITMENT").

### Form Fields & Inputs
- Matte obsidian container (`#070A11`), 1px muted border (`rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.2)`).
- Focus state: Border switches sharply to `#00D2FF` with a hairline exterior cyan glow (`0 0 0 1px #00D2FF`). Text in `#FFFFFF`. Placeholder text in `#64748B`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Sharp 2px rounded square containers with high-contrast active states.
- Active state: Filled with `#00D2FF`, displaying a crisp `#05070B` geometric checkmark. Inactive state features a dark obsidian bed with a ghost border.

### Press & Careers Lists
- Modular interactive strips separated by 1px rules (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`).
- Hover highlights the entire row with a translucent wash (`rgba(0, 210, 255, 0.03)`) and reveals an electric-blue left anchor line (2px width).
